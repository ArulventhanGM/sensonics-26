About EAREYES '26

Earreyes is a premier Inter Department Level Technical Symposium organized annually by the Department of Electronics and Instrumentation Engineering. Designed to cultivate technological innovation, engineering excellence, and multi-disciplinary talent, Eareyes provides a dynamic platform for students across the nation to present innovative ideas, solve engineering challenges, and gain hands-on expertise in automation, sensing technology, and robotics.
